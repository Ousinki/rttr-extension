# Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** May 2026

Welcome to **RTTR — RubyText Translator** (the "Extension"). This Privacy Policy explains how we handle your data when you use the Extension. We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring transparency.

## 1. Information We Access

The Extension requires the following minimal permissions to function:

- **`activeTab` (Read and change your data on the websites you visit):**
  The Extension only accesses the text content of the specific web page you are currently viewing when you actively trigger a translation (e.g., by clicking a word, using a keyboard shortcut, or hovering over a paragraph). We do **not** monitor, record, or track your browsing history in the background.

- **`storage` (Storage):**
  The Extension stores your preferences (such as translation engine choice, display settings) and your personal API keys (if configured) securely in your browser's local storage.

## 2. How We Handle Your Data

- **API Keys are Stored Locally:** Your API keys are saved locally on your device. We do **not** have access to your keys, and they are never transmitted to our servers.
- **Third-Party Data Processing:** To provide translations, the text you select or hover over is securely transmitted directly from your browser to the third-party translation or AI service you have configured (e.g., Google Translate, DeepL, Google Gemini, OpenAI, or other chosen LLM providers). 
- **No Data Collection:** We do not own, operate, or maintain any telemetry servers. We do not collect, store, sell, or share any of your personal information, browsing data, or translated text.

## 3. Third-Party Services

When using the Extension, your text is processed according to the Privacy Policies of the translation engines and AI services you configure:
- [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy)
- [DeepL Privacy Policy](https://www.deepl.com/privacy)
- [OpenAI Privacy Policy](https://openai.com/policies/privacy-policy)

## 4. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page. By continuing to use the Extension, you agree to the latest terms.

## 5. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact the developer via the GitHub repository where this project is hosted.

# 隐私政策

**生效日期：** 2026年5月

欢迎使用 **RTTR — RubyText Translator**（以下简称“本插件”）。本隐私政策旨在向您说明在您使用本插件时，我们如何处理您的数据。我们承诺保护您的隐私并保持透明。

## 1. 我们访问的信息

本插件仅需要以下最低限度的权限以维持正常运行：

- **`activeTab` (读取和更改您在访问的网站上的数据)：**
  仅当您主动触发翻译功能（如：点击单词、使用快捷键或鼠标悬停在段落上）时，本插件才会访问您当前所浏览网页的文本内容。我们**不会**在后台监控、记录或追踪您的浏览历史。

- **`storage` (存储)：**
  本插件会将您的偏好设置（如：翻译引擎选择、显示设置）以及您配置的个人 API 密钥安全地存储在您浏览器的本地存储（Local Storage）中。

## 2. 数据处理方式

- **API 密钥本地存储：** 您的 API 密钥仅保存在您的本地设备上。我们**无法**获取您的密钥，密钥也绝不会被发送至我们的私人服务器。
- **第三方数据处理：** 为了实现翻译功能，您选中或悬停的文本将直接从您的浏览器安全传输至您配置的第三方翻译或 AI 服务（如：Google Translate、DeepL、Google Gemini、OpenAI 或其他大语言模型提供商）。
- **零数据收集：** 我们没有、也不运营任何用于收集用户数据的服务器。我们不会收集、存储、出售或共享您的任何个人信息、浏览数据或翻译文本。

## 3. 第三方服务

使用本插件时，您的文本将受您所配置的翻译引擎或 AI 服务提供商的隐私政策约束：
- [Google 隐私政策](https://policies.google.com/privacy)
- [DeepL 隐私政策](https://www.deepl.com/privacy)
- [OpenAI 隐私政策](https://openai.com/policies/privacy-policy)

## 4. 政策变更

我们可能会不时更新本隐私政策。任何变更都将在此页面公示。继续使用本插件即表示您同意最新版本的条款。

## 5. 联系我们

如果您对本隐私政策有任何疑问，请通过托管本项目的 GitHub 仓库联系开发者。
